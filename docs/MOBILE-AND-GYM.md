# Cube Coach — iPhone version + Middle Layer Gym (build spec, Nov 2026)

Read ARCHITECTURE.md first (module contracts, conventions, the method we teach, "As built" notes).
This file adds two things. Where they conflict, this file wins for its own scope.

## Part A — the iPhone version

The learner wants Cube Coach on his iPhone, in **Chrome for iOS** (WebKit underneath, like Safari).
Decision: host the same app on **GitHub Pages** at `https://pmcgroark.github.io/cube-coach/` (https
⇒ camera, storage and service workers work), installable with **Add to Home Screen** (app icon,
standalone window) and **working offline** after the first open. One codebase serves laptop and
phone; the local `serve.py` setup keeps working for development.

Requirements:

1. **Relative paths only** (the site lives under `/cube-coach/`). No absolute `/js/...` URLs.
2. **No CDN at runtime**: vendor three.js r128 as `vendor/three.min.js` (keep its MIT licence
   header) and load it locally, so the service worker can cache it.
3. **PWA**: `manifest.webmanifest` (name "Cube Coach", short_name "Cube Coach", start_url ".",
   scope ".", display "standalone", theme/background from the CSS tokens, icons 192/512 + maskable),
   `apple-touch-icon` (180 px PNG), `apple-mobile-web-app-capable`, status-bar style, title. Icons are
   generated by a dependency-free node script `tools/make-icons.js` (zlib PNG writer) from the brand
   mark (the 3×3 cube in index.html).
4. **Service worker** `sw.js` at the site root: precache every app file (index.html, css, all js,
   vendor, icons, manifest) under a versioned cache name; cache-first for same-origin GETs; navigation
   requests fall back to the cached `index.html`; old caches deleted on activate. When a new version
   is waiting, the app shows a toast "A new version is ready — Reload". Register it from app.js only on
   https origins (NOT on localhost/127.0.0.1, so development with serve.py never serves stale files),
   unless `?sw=1` is in the URL. A node test asserts every `<script src>`/stylesheet in index.html and
   every file in js/ is in the precache list.
5. **Phone ergonomics** (iPhone 13/14/15 = 390×844 CSS px is the main target; also 375×667 and
   430×932): bottom tab bar on phones (Home · Learn · Practice · Stats · Settings) respecting
   `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; content padded for the notch/home indicator; tap targets ≥ 44 px;
   `touch-action: manipulation` on controls (no double-tap zoom); the 3D cube canvas uses
   `touch-action: none` only while dragging the cube, page scroll elsewhere; no horizontal scroll;
   inputs ≥ 16 px font (prevents iOS zoom-on-focus); landscape works too.
6. **Keep the screen awake** during a solve, a scan and gym drills: `CC.App.keepAwake(true|false)`
   using the Screen Wake Lock API (re-acquire on visibilitychange; silently no-op where unsupported).
7. **Camera on a phone**: default to the **rear camera** (`facingMode: 'environment'`) on phones, with
   a "Switch camera" button; mirror the preview only for the front camera; remember the choice
   (`settings.cameraFacing`). The scan instructions must make sense when the phone is pointed at the
   cube ("Point the camera at the GREEN centre, WHITE on top"). The raw frame is still the face seen
   head-on, so the facelet mapping is unchanged — verify. `playsinline` + `muted` stay on the video.
8. **Moving progress between laptop and phone** (different devices/origins): Settings › "Move my
   progress": *Copy progress code* (compact base64 of the store JSON, prefixed `CUBE1.`, survives
   being pasted through Messages/WhatsApp: whitespace, line breaks, smart quotes stripped) and *Paste a
   code* with a preview and a choice of **Merge** (solves union by id; skills keep the higher mastery and
   the larger session count; drills/gym counts keep the larger totals; practiceLog per day keeps the
   larger minutes; milestones earliest date; algs keep the higher box) or **Replace**. Also keep the
   existing JSON backup download/upload.
9. **Settings › "On your phone"**: the hosted link with Copy/Share buttons, step-by-step "Add to Home
   Screen" instructions for Chrome on iPhone (Share ⇧ → Add to Home Screen) and Safari, and an
   "Offline ready ✓" status once the service worker has cached everything.

## Part B — the Middle Layer Gym

The learner is stuck on stage 4 (`middle`) and named **all four** problems:

1. **Remembering the moves** — the two 8-move sequences; mixing up left and right.
2. **Seeing what to do** — spotting the right edge, lining up the upside-down T, deciding left/right.
3. **Stuck edges** — an edge in the middle layer in the wrong slot or flipped; no usable edge on top.
4. **Losing my place** — the white layer gets broken, or he loses track partway through a sequence.

The Gym (`#/gym`) is a dedicated practice area with many short, focused exercises, phone-first, most
of them doable without a cube (fast taps), plus "do it on the virtual cube" and "do it on your real
cube" variants. Learning frame throughout: **yellow on top, white on the bottom** (as in the method),
cube shown as held. Algorithms:

* **Right** (edge goes to the front-right slot): `U R U' R' U' F' U F`
* **Left**  (edge goes to the front-left slot):  `U' L' U L U F U' F'`

Teach each as **two halves of four moves** and teach **left as the mirror of right** (swap R↔L, flip
every direction) so there is one thing to remember, not two. Every claim about what a move or half
does (where the corner and edge are after the first half, which way the edge goes, etc.) must be
**derived by simulation with CC.Cube**, never assumed.

### Groups and drills (`CC.Gym.DRILLS`)

| Group | Drill id | What it trains | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 See it | `spot` | which top edges belong in the middle layer (no yellow) — or "none: pop one out" | tap the edges on a top-layer diagram (multi-select) |
| | `lineup` | which top turn (U, U', U2, none) puts the highlighted edge above its matching centre (the upside-down T), cube held with that centre in front | 4 buttons |
| | `leftright` | with the T made, does the edge go left or right (its top colour = the side centre it goes to) | ← / → , speed round with streak |
| | `decide` | the whole decision from a random top layer: which edge → hold which centre in front → which turn → left or right | 3 quick taps in sequence |
| 2 Remember it | `halves` | learn right/left in two halves with a rhythm and plain English; watch each half animate | guided walkthrough, predict the next move |
| | `nextmove` | after the first k moves, what comes next? | 4 options, both algorithms, random k |
| | `mirror` | build the left algorithm from the right (and back) | tap 8 moves from a pad |
| | `recall` | perform the algorithm on the virtual cube with fading cues: all moves shown → every other move hidden → first move only → nothing | virtual cube (swipe / move pad / keyboard) |
| 3 Stuck edges | `stuck-spot` | is this edge stuck? (wrong slot / flipped in its own slot / fine) | 3 buttons on a 3D view with the slot highlighted |
| | `stuck-fix` | no usable top edge but a bad middle edge: how to pop it out (hold that slot at front-right, do the Right algorithm with any top edge), then do it | choose, then perform on the virtual cube |
| 4 Keep your place | `landmark` | what the cube should look like after the first half and after the whole algorithm (white layer broken mid-way is normal) | pick the right picture from 3 |
| | `whereami` | a cube mid-algorithm: how many moves have been done / what is the next move? | 4 options |
| | `slip` | one wrong move was made (e.g. R instead of R'): undo it and finish correctly | virtual cube, target state checked |
| | `slowmo` | real-cube companion: big one-move-at-a-time player with a checkpoint picture after each half so you can check you're on track | real cube |
| 5 Put it together | `full-virtual` | solve all four middle edges on the virtual cube with hints on request | opens `solve` with `{device:'virtual', source:'drill', stage:'middle'}` |
| | `full-real` | a scramble that only disturbs the middle layer, for your real cube, then coached solve | opens `solve` with `{device:'physical', source:'drill', stage:'middle'}` |
| | `sprint` | four edges on the virtual cube, timed, no hints; best times | virtual cube |

### `CC.Gym` (js/gym.js — pure, node-testable)

```
CC.Gym.ALGS = { right: { moves:[...], halves:[{ moves, name, plain:[per-move English], rhythm }], mirrorOf:'left' },
                left:  { … } }
CC.Gym.DRILLS = [{ id, group:1..5, groupName, name, desc, kind:'tap'|'multi'|'sequence'|'perform'|'walkthrough'|'route',
                   needsCube:false|'virtual'|'real', route?:{ id, params } }]
CC.Gym.next(drillId, { seed, level? }) -> Item
Item = { drillId, prompt, state (54, as held, learning frame), view:'3d'|'top'|'both', highlight:[sticker indices],
         options:[{ id, label, sub?, state? }] | null, answer: id | [ids] | null,
         steps?: [{ prompt, options, answer }]            // 'decide' (sequence of questions)
         moves?: [tokens]                                  // intended solution for perform/walkthrough
         cues?: [bool per move]                            // which moves are shown (recall)
         targetState?: [54], explain, alg?: 'right'|'left' }
CC.Gym.check(item, response) -> { correct, explain, partial? }   // response: option id(s) or the cube state after the learner's moves
CC.Gym.session(drillId, { seed, count:10 }) -> [Item]       // no duplicates in a round
CC.Gym.LANDMARKS = { right:[{ after:4, text }, { after:8, text }], left:[…] }  // derived and asserted by tests
```

Generators build realistic states from a solved cube in the learning frame
(`CC.Cube.applyAlg(CC.Cube.solved(),'z2')`): scramble the last layer with layer-preserving sequences,
un-insert middle edges with inverse algorithms (and flips/wrong-slot swaps for stuck cases), then
rotate with y so practice isn't always from the same side. Tests run every drill over ≥ 500 seeds and
check answers by simulation (e.g. applying the chosen U turn really makes the T; the stated algorithm
really inserts the edge and keeps the first layer; stuck cases really are stuck; landmarks hold).

### Progress

`data.gym = { [drillId]: { right, total, streak, best, bestMs, lastAt, recent:[0/1…] } }` (the store
keeps unknown keys). Each answer also calls `CC.Coach.recordDrill(data, 'gym-' + drillId, correct)`.
A drill shows "Strong" at ≥ 80 % of the last 10. The hub suggests the next drill from the weakest
group. Full middle-layer solves go through the solve screen, which already records stage mastery.

### Screen (`#/gym`, js/screens/gym.js) and the top-layer diagram (js/topview.js)

* Hub: the four problems as cards in the learner's words ("I can't remember the moves", "I don't
  know what to do", "An edge is stuck", "I lose my place") + "Put it together", each listing its
  drills with progress; a "Start with this" suggestion.
* Runner: 10-item rounds, one question at a time, big thumb-friendly answers, instant feedback with
  the explanation and "Show me" (animates on the 3D cube), progress dots, streak, end-of-round summary.
* `CC.TopView.render(container, state, { highlight, selectable:[positions], onSelect, size })`: an
  SVG of the top face (3×3) with the top row of each side face around it (the standard last-layer
  diagram), front at the bottom; edges tappable when selectable. Pure geometry helpers testable in node.
* Entry points: Home (a tile when the Middle Layer is the current/weakest stage), the Middle Layer
  lesson ("Go to the gym"), Practice › Stages (middle row), and the solve screen while on the middle
  stage ("Stuck? Practise in the Gym").

## As built (Nov 2026)

What was actually built, where it differs from or adds to the spec above. ARCHITECTURE.md has the
full module contracts; the code (each file's header and exports) is the final word.

### Part A — the iPhone version

* **Hosting.** Live at `https://pmcgroark.github.io/cube-coach/` (`CC.App.HOSTED_URL`), served by
  GitHub Pages from the `main` branch, root folder, with `.nojekyll`. three.js r128 is
  `vendor/three.min.js`; nothing is fetched from a CDN.
* **Service worker.** `CC.App.swDecision(location)` decides: `register` on https (or `?sw=1` on
  localhost), `dev` on localhost / 127.0.0.1 / `*.localhost` (never cache; any old copy is removed, and a page that
  came from it reloads once), `off` with `?sw=0`, `insecure` on plain http elsewhere, `unavailable` for
  file://. The update toast reads "A new version is ready —" with a **Reload** button
  (`CC.App.applyUpdate()` → `SKIP_WAITING` → reload). A Home Screen app also checks for a new version
  when it comes back to the front (at most every 30 minutes), and the app asks for persistent storage.
  State changes fire a `cc:sw` window event; `CC.App.offlineStatus()` asks the worker for
  `{ ready, cached, total, version }`.
* **Precache list and version are generated:** `node tests/platform.test.js --stamp` writes `PRECACHE`
  (index.html, manifest, everything under css/, js/, vendor/, icons/) and `VERSION` (date + content
  hash) into sw.js. Run it before every publish: installed phones only fetch new files when sw.js
  changes. The platform test fails if a file is missing and prints a NOTE when files changed since
  the last stamp. dev/ pages are not precached.
* **Icons.** `tools/make-icons.js` also writes `icons/qr-cube-coach.svg`, a QR code of the hosted
  link, shown in Settings › On your phone on laptops only. `--check` exits 1 when the icons are stale.
* **Phone layout.** Bottom tab bar at ≤ 720 px wide; on a phone on its side (≤ 720 px wide and
  ≤ 500 px high) the tabs move back into the top bar so the cube keeps the height. Toasts sit above
  the tab bar. The solve screen docks its controls above the tab bar on a portrait phone and goes
  two-column on its side.
* **Touch on the 3D cube.** Instead of `touch-action: none` while dragging, a draggable cube's canvas
  is `touch-action: pan-y`: touches that land on the cube's outline (plus a 12 px margin) are claimed
  and turn/orbit the cube; touches on the background around it scroll the page vertically.
* **Keep awake.** `CC.App.keepAwake(on, reason)`: the screen stays on while any reason is on; every
  route change clears all reasons (screens ask again in `mount()`); `keepAwake(false)` with no reason
  clears everything. Reasons used: `'solve'`, `'scan'` (kept inside the rescan dialog too, so closing
  it leaves the solve's lock alone) and `'gym'` (while a round runs). `CC.App.isAwake()` reports it.
* **Camera.** `settings.cameraFacing` is `null` (automatic: rear on phones via
  `CC.Scanner.defaultFacing()`, front elsewhere), `'environment'` or `'user'`. It is set by
  **Switch camera** on the scan screen and by Settings › Camera; choosing a facing clears
  `cameraId`/`cameraLabel`, and choosing a named camera whose label says front/back sets the facing.
  The instructions follow the camera in use (`CC.Scanner.sayFor`: "Point the camera at the GREEN
  centre" with the rear camera, where the next face is on the learner's RIGHT). Verified: the raw
  frame and the facelet mapping are the same for both cameras (tests/scanner.test.js). The controller
  reports `onState` ('starting' / 'live' / 'paused' / 'blocked' / 'idle'), releases the camera while
  the page is hidden and reopens it on return; 'blocked' needs a tap (`resume()`).
* **Progress codes.** `CUBE1.` + base64 of a zlib stream (a small built-in deflate). About 40,000
  characters after 300 solves (measured). `CC.Store.merge`: solves union by id; skills higher mastery,
  larger session/soloClean counts, latest practice date; drills and gym: the record with the larger
  total, plus the higher best streak and the lower `bestMs` for gym; practiceLog per day the larger
  minutes; milestones earliest; algs higher box. Device settings never travel
  (`CC.Store.DEVICE_SETTINGS`: theme, mirror, autoCapture, cameraId, cameraLabel, cameraFacing,
  keyboard). Merge and Replace can be undone from the toast that follows. A pasted code is checked
  as soon as it looks complete.
* **Settings › On your phone** also shows a "Your browser" badge on the matching steps card and, in
  the Home Screen app, "You're using the Home Screen app ✓". `go('settings', { section })` scrolls to
  a card (`phone`, `move`, `look`, `coach`, `keys`, `camera`, `timer`, `data`, `about`).

### Part B — the Middle Layer Gym

* **Drills as built.** 17 drills as in the table, with these differences: `decide` is **four** taps
  (which edge → which centre in front → which top turn → left or right); `stuck-fix` offers four ways
  (only "front-right, Right algorithm" is right) and then has you do it; `landmark` shows three
  pictures drawn with `CC.TopView.renderIso` (one WebGL cube per screen); `mirror` goes from single
  moves to halves to the whole algorithm; `recall` fades the cues over a round (levels
  1,1,2,2,3,3,3,4,4,4); `halves` shows the first move of each half at level 1.
* **Feedback.** Every wrong option carries a `why` worked out by simulation; `CC.Gym.check()` puts it
  first in `explain`. Wording uses plain English, British spelling and curly quotes.
* **Round lengths.** 10 items; `halves` and `slowmo` 4; `sprint` 3. `go('gym', { drill })` with a
  route drill (`full-virtual`, `full-real`) goes straight to the solve screen.
* **Perform drills** (`recall`, `slip`, `stuck-fix`) count as right only within the clean number of
  face turns + 6 (rotations don't count); `stuck-fix` also needs the right choice first. The goal
  check holds the cube yellow-up first, needs the white layer whole and earlier edges still home;
  `popout` accepts the edge out on top **or already put back home properly**. The `sprint` counts
  whenever all four edges are in and records the time.
* **Progress.** `data.gym[drillId] = { right, total, streak, best, bestMs, bestRoundMs?, lastAt,
  recent:[last 10] }`. `bestMs` is the best sprint time (only the sprint passes a time).
  `bestRoundMs` is the best **perfect** 10/10 round of `leftright` (`CC.Gym.recordRound`). Each answer
  also records `CC.Coach.recordDrill(data, 'gym-' + drillId, correct)`; Stats folds those rows into
  one "Middle Layer Gym" row. "Strong" = 8 of the last 10 right.
* **Suggestion.** `CC.Gym.suggest(data, { hasCube })`: the weakest of groups 1–4, then its weakest
  drill that is not strong yet; ties follow `CC.Gym.PRIORITY = [2, 1, 3, 4]` (remembering the moves
  first, the learner's own order), so a new learner starts with "Learn it in halves". Everything
  strong: the sprint (or the real-cube middle layer with a cube).
* **Screen.** The hub shows "Start with this", a fold-out card with both algorithms in halves, the
  groups in PRIORITY order and then "Put it together". In a round, left-algorithm items are shown
  from the front-left and the stuck-fix slot from its own side. "Show me" animates the answer on the
  3D cube; the round ends with a score, best times, "Worth another look" (the missed questions),
  **Another round** and **Next: …** (the new suggestion).
* **Top-layer diagram.** `CC.TopView.render(container, state, { highlight, selectable, selected, marks,
  onSelect, size, caption, label })` → `{ el, update, focus, destroy }`, plus
  `CC.TopView.renderIso(container, state, { side, highlight, dim, size, label, caption })`.
* **Lesson.** `CC.Lessons.STAGES.middle` gained `halves`, `mirrorRule`, `whyItWorks`, `landmarks` and
  `lostPlace`, shown as cards on the lesson page, and there are 9 middle demos (right, left, stuck,
  edge at the back, not above its own colour, two in a row, wrong slot, no usable edge, the
  yellow-edge trap).
* **Entry points as built:** Home tile (middle is the focus stage, or the weakest practised stage
  below Solo, or the gym was used in the last 14 days); the Middle Layer lesson and its stage card;
  Practice › Stages; Stats (Drill accuracy links to the weakest gym drill); the solve screen on the
  middle stage ("Stuck? Practise in the Gym", which asks before leaving a solve in progress).

### Resolved after the QA pass

* `CC.View3D.setView(side)` exists; the Gym calls it (synthetic drag only as a fallback).
* `CC.Store.merge` keeps the faster `bestRoundMs` of two devices.
* `CC.Coach.todayPlan` adds a 5-minute `kind:'gym'` item (with `drillId` from `CC.Gym.suggest`) while the
  middle layer has been tried and its mastery is under the Nudge threshold (50); Home routes it to
  `go('gym', { drill })` and ticks it when that drill (or any gym drill) was used since the plan was made.
